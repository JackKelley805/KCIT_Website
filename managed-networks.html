Secure and dependable internet access

We deploy professionally configured firewall systems to protect your network at the edge. Through VLAN-based network segmentation, advanced firewall policies, and intrusion protection systems, we isolate devices, control traffic flow, and help defend business networks from unauthorized access and malicious activity.

WAN Failover & Redundant Connectivity

Networks can be engineered with WAN failover to provide redundancy in the event of an internet outage. Firewall and routing systems automatically detect connection failures and shift traffic to a secondary internet connection, helping maintain connectivity for critical systems. Backup connectivity options may be available through Cygnus Communications, depending on service availability.
